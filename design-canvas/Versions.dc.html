Migration plan: nightly batch to streaming

The nightly batch has been the reconciliation path since 2019, and every incident review since March has ended at the same sentence: the ledger is correct by morning and wrong all afternoon.

We will cut over in one weekend and keep a rollback script ready.

We keep the batch running in parallel for two full quarters. It costs about forty euros a month in compute and it is the only way to answer the question an auditor will actually ask, which is whether the two paths ever disagreed.

Rollout

  1. Shadow writes, no reads. Four weeks.
  2. Reads behind a per-tenant flag, starting with internal tenants.
